About Us

For more than two decades, Aditya Kamaraja Hospitals has been a familiar and trusted presence in the community, growing alongside the families we have had the privilege of caring for.

Founded in 2001 by Dr. V. Kamaraja, M.D. and Dr. N. Rajeswari, M.S., the hospital was built on a simple belief: that healthcare is not only about treating an illness, but about making every patient feel cared for, understood and safe.

Today, we carry forward the legacy of Dr. V. Kamaraja, whose dedication and presence remain an enduring part of the hospital's story. Though he is no longer with us, the values he helped build into AKH continue to live on in the way we care for our patients and serve our community.

What began as a specialty clinic has grown into a 20-bed multi-speciality hospital, with both inpatient and outpatient facilities. Through every stage of that journey, one thing has remained unchanged: our commitment to personal and individual attention.

At the heart of our journey is Aditya Kamaraja Eye Clinic, where generations of patients have sought care for their vision and eye health. For us, eye care is about more than treating a condition. It is about helping someone see a loved one's face clearly, read a book comfortably, move through life with confidence, and preserve their independence.

Today, AKH brings together general and specialty consultations and surgical care under one roof, allowing patients to access different areas of healthcare while retaining the personal attention that has always been central to our approach.

We are grateful for the trust that families from our neighbourhood and beyond have placed in us over the years. That trust is one of the most meaningful parts of our journey.

Behind every patient is a team that makes this care possible. Our trained nurses and operational staff are an integral part of the hospital. Through continuous learning and development, we strive to provide care with knowledge, confidence, compassion and dedication.

From a specialty clinic to a multi-speciality hospital, our journey has always been about people.

And as we continue to grow, our commitment remains the same: to care for every patient with the attention, reassurance and human warmth they deserve.

Frequently asked questions

What departments are available at AKH?

Aditya Kamaraja Hospitals is a multi-speciality hospital. The main pathways are Eye Care (Aditya Kamaraja Eye Clinic), Integrated Sleep Care and General Medicine, along with specialist departments: General Surgery and Surgical Gastroenterology, Pain Management and Orthopaedics, Pulmonology, ENT, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Urology, Neurology and Plastic Surgery. Inpatient and outpatient care are both available.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the Book a Consultation button on any page to send us a request with your name, phone number and area of concern, and our team will call you to confirm a time. You can also call or WhatsApp 81220 01809.

Do I need an appointment?

It depends on the department. Eye Care welcomes walk-ins during consultation hours, 10 am to 1 pm and 6 to 9 pm, all days except Tuesday. Integrated Sleep Care welcomes walk-ins and also offers online consultations. General Medicine welcomes walk-ins on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 6:30 PM to 8:00 PM. All other specialties are by appointment only.

Are walk-ins available?

Yes, for Eye Care, Integrated Sleep Care and General Medicine during their consultation hours. For the other specialties, please book an appointment so that the specialist is available when you come.

Are online consultations available?

Online consultations are available for sleep-related concerns through Integrated Sleep Care, so you can consult our sleep physicians from the comfort of your home. Other departments see patients in person.

How can I contact AKH?

Call or WhatsApp 81220 01809. You can also send a consultation request through the form on this website, and our team will get back to you.

Where is AKH located?

Aditya Kamaraja Hospitals is at 36, 1st Main Road, Alwarthirunagar Annexe, Virugambakkam, Chennai 600092, near Dentistree Dental Care.

How do I reach the hospital?

We are on 1st Main Road in Alwarthirunagar Annexe, a few minutes from Valasaravakkam, Vadapalani and Porur. The Contact page has a map and a directions link that opens in Google Maps.

How can I enquire about a specific specialist?

Call or WhatsApp 81220 01809, or send a consultation request and choose the department under area of concern. Our team will confirm the specialist's availability and a suitable time with you.

Does AKH have a 24-hour emergency service?

No. AKH does not run a 24-hour emergency service. In a medical emergency, please go to the nearest emergency service without delay.
