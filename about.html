About Us

Care. Comfort. Commitment.

The story of Aditya Kamaraja Hospitals, a multi-speciality hospital in Alwarthirunagar, Chennai, caring for families since 2001.

For more than two decades, Aditya Kamaraja Hospitals has been a familiar and trusted presence in the community, growing alongside the families we have had the privilege of caring for.

Founded in 2001 by Dr. V. Kamaraja, M.D. and Dr. N. Rajeswari, M.S., the hospital was built on a simple belief: that healthcare is not only about treating an illness, but about making every patient feel cared for, understood and safe.

Today, we carry forward the legacy of Dr. V. Kamaraja, whose dedication and presence remain an enduring part of the hospital's story. Though he is no longer with us, the values he helped build into AKH continue to live on in the way we care for our patients and serve our community.

What began as a specialty clinic has grown into a 20-bed multi-speciality hospital, with both inpatient and outpatient facilities. Through every stage of that journey, one thing has remained unchanged: our commitment to personal and individual attention.

At the heart of our journey is Aditya Kamaraja Eye Clinic, where generations of patients have sought care for their vision and eye health. For us, eye care is about more than treating a condition. It is about helping someone see a loved one's face clearly, read a book comfortably, move through life with confidence, and preserve their independence.

Today, AKH brings together general and specialty consultations and surgical care under one roof, allowing patients to access different areas of healthcare while retaining the personal attention that has always been central to our approach.

We are grateful for the trust that families from our neighbourhood and beyond have placed in us over the years. That trust is one of the most meaningful parts of our journey.

Behind every patient is a team that makes this care possible. Our trained nurses and operational staff are an integral part of the hospital. Through continuous learning and development, we strive to provide care with knowledge, confidence, compassion and dedication.

From a specialty clinic to a multi-speciality hospital, our journey has always been about people.

And as we continue to grow, our commitment remains the same: to care for every patient with the attention, reassurance and human warmth they deserve.
